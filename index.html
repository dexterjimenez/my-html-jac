Good questions. Clear answers.

A few things
before you ship.

Your first shipment? Planning something bigger? We’re here to help you take the next step.

Talk to our team
How do I shop in the USA and ship to the Philippines?
Start by setting up your account and confirming your Johnny Air U.S. receiving address and customer reference. Shop from your chosen U.S. store or supplier and use the confirmed shipping instructions. Johnny Air receives and processes your shipment for onward shipping to the Philippines. Confirm delivery or pickup arrangements with the team.
Can I combine purchases from different U.S. stores?
Ask Johnny Air about package consolidation for your purchases. Confirm item eligibility, receiving instructions, any applicable charges and the shipping arrangement before sending multiple packages.
Should I choose air freight or sea freight?
The right option depends on your cargo, dimensions, budget and timing needs. Ask the team to confirm current service availability, schedules and requirements for your shipment. Request a quote to discuss both options.
How much will my shipment cost?
A quote depends on the shipment details, including weight, dimensions, contents, shipping method and destination. Johnny Air must confirm current rates and applicable charges. The shipping calculator does not invent or assume a shipping rate.
Are there items I should check before buying?
Yes. Always confirm that your item is eligible for the intended service before purchasing or sending it. Share a product description and product link with the team, and review the current prohibited items information and shipping guidelines.
